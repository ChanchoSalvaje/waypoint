# Waypoint — Scoring Rubric (v1)

> Two scores rank every opportunity. **Fit** is the tool's assessment; **Excitement** is yours.
> Documented here so the numbers mean something, and surfaced as a "?" legend in the app.

## Fit Score (1–5) — tool-assessed
How well the role matches your target criteria and hard filters. Set by mining/enrichment (read-only in the app).
- **5 — Bullseye:** identity-match role; clears all hard filters; strong lane + level alignment.
- **4 — Strong:** clear fit with one minor gap or unknown to verify.
- **3 — Plausible:** adjacent or possible, but real gaps/unknowns (lane, level, comp, or location).
- **2 — Weak:** significant mismatch on lane, level, or a filter; long shot.
- **1 — Poor:** fails a core criterion; likely drop.

Hard filters (a fail caps Fit low regardless of everything else):
Remote-US or Tucson · comp ≥ floor · no banking/finance (insurance OK) · no active-clearance / defense.

## Excitement (1–5) — self-rated
Your gut pull toward the role, independent of fit. Only you set this.
- **5 — Thrilled:** dream-adjacent.
- **4 — Energized:** genuinely want it.
- **3 — Interested:** neutral-positive.
- **2 — Lukewarm:** would take it if needed.
- **1 — Flat:** pragmatic-only, no pull.

## Reading them together
- **High Fit + High Excitement** → top priority; pursue hard.
- **High Fit + Low Excitement** → worth a look; ask *why* the low pull (comp? mission? role shape?).
- **Low Fit + High Excitement** → passion pull; pursue with clear eyes on the gaps.
- **Low Fit + Low Excitement** → drop; don't spend cycles.
